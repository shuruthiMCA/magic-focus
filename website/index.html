✦ WHAT YOU GET ✦

⏱️

Focus timer

A calm timer with goals for work, school and exam study, plus a small always-visible mini timer.

🧩

4 brain games

Memory Match, Quick Math, Word Scramble and a logic quiz to refresh your mind on breaks.

📚

Flashcards

Add your own questions and answers and revise them until you know them.

🎁

Rewards

Earn XP, crystals, streaks and mystery gifts every time you finish a focus session.

🎨

Themes and avatars

Unlock new looks with the crystals you earn and make the app your own.

🌊

Relaxing sounds

Ocean, calm and zen sounds to help you settle into your work.
